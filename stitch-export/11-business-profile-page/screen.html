Founder's Journey & Growth Story

In October 2021, Nusrat Jahan converted her domestic 28-liter countertop oven in Dhanmondi into a weekend cottage bakery, selling hand-whisked Belgian brownies to classmates and neighborhood Facebook groups.

Through relentless obsession with European raw chocolate sourcing and punctuality, word-of-mouth spread across corporate hubs in Gulshan and Banani. Today, Nusrat's Home Kitchen regularly prepares over 450 gourmet snack and gift hampers every month for corporate farewells, Eid distributions, and tech startups.
